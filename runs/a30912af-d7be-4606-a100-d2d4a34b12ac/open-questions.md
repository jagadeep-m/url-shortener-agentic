# Open questions

Requirement: Make the links more secure and give marketing better analytics.

Answer each question on its "Answer:" line with one or more option letters (for example "A" or "A, C"), or "Other: <your answer>". Save the file, then approve. Changing an answer later re-plans the stages that depend on it.

## Q1. Which links should be made more secure?
- A. Links shared with customers, such as public pages or campaign URLs.
- B. Links that grant access or perform actions, such as sign-in, password-reset, or invitation links.
- C. All links in the product, including internal and administrative links.
Answer: A

## Q2. What security improvements are required for those links?
- A. Prevent tampering or interception, for example by requiring HTTPS and signing or expiring sensitive URLs.
- B. Limit access, for example with authentication, permissions, or one-time use.
- C. Detect and block unsafe destinations, such as phishing or malware URLs.
Answer: B

## Q3. What should marketing analytics measure?
- A. Campaign performance, such as visits, conversions, and attribution by campaign.
- B. User engagement, such as clicks, page views, and repeat visits.
- C. Business outcomes, such as leads, purchases, or revenue attributed to marketing.
Answer: C

## Q4. Where should marketing analytics be available?
- A. In a new reporting dashboard within the product.
- B. In an existing analytics or business-intelligence system.
- C. As downloadable reports or data exports.
Answer: C

## Q5. What user data may be collected for analytics, and under what privacy constraints?
- A. Aggregate, non-identifying event counts only.
- B. Pseudonymous user or session-level activity, subject to consent and retention limits.
- C. Identifiable customer activity where permitted by applicable policy and law.
Answer: B

## Q6. What platforms and links are in scope for the change?
- A. The public website only.
- B. The website and mobile applications.
- C. All customer-facing and internal products, including integrations.
Answer: B

## Answers
- Q1: A
- Q2: B
- Q3: C
- Q4: C
- Q5: B
- Q6: B
